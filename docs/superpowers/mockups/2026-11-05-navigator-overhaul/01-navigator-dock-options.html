Navigator overhaul — dock structure options

Both frames show the same end state required by #1355: every left panel pulled up to the top-left stack, the map as a floating panel over the canvas (toolbar toggle, movable, lockable, layer visibility), a node list driven by canvas-note grouping, and a relations view driven by the current selection. The difference is only how the navigator area is organised. Click the icons on the left edge in each frame to switch panels. The lock and layers buttons on the map work.

Option A recommended — two independent panels
"Find nodes" and "Relations" are two separate dock panels with their own icons, like every other panel. They are used at different moments (searching vs. tracing), so they are toggled independently. One dock slot each, no nesting.
