Prototype 04 — Floating map (minimap)

The map leaves the dock and floats over the canvas. Drag its header to move it, resize from the bottom-right corner, lock it in place, or hide it entirely via the toolbar toggle (like today's dock panel visibility). Its layers (nodes / edges / canvas notes) toggle from its header. The viewport rectangle is live: drag the canvas to pan and the rectangle follows; drag the rectangle inside the map to pan the canvas. Position, size, visibility and layers are all part of the persisted settings (shown on the right).
