Prototype 03 — "Find" panel

Search bar + node tree, grouped by canvas notes. The canvas on the right is live: drag nodes in and out of notes and the grouping updates. Notes that overlap a node at its center claim it; a node can belong to several notes and then appears under each. Nodes in no note land in the Ungrouped root group. Other compartments only show up while a search is active.
